# CLIMBER PR — Live Segments Offline

**Live climb & segment timing for your Hammerhead Karoo — fully offline, no subscription.**

CLIMBER PR times the segments you ride and races you against your own personal
record, live, on the screen — everything runs on the device, no data connection
needed. The first time, it records the segment's signature and your time. Next
time you ride it, the data field shows a live delta against your best attempt:
**−0:05** in green if you're ahead, **+0:10** in red if you're behind, plus the
distance left to the finish.

> Works while recording a ride. Add the **Live Segment** data field to any screen
> profile after installing (it appears under the *Live Segments Offline* extension).

## Create your own segments — right from the bike

You're not limited to climbs. **Mark any stretch of road as a segment while you
ride**, using the Karoo's **Lap button**:

1. Press **Lap** where you want the segment to **start** (point A).
2. Press **Lap** again where you want it to **finish** (point B).

That's it — no route planning, no computer, no climb required. It works for a flat
sprint, a descent, your local loop, anything. The segment is saved on the device.

**From then on it's automatic.** Every time you ride through that start point in the
same A → B direction, CLIMBER PR starts timing on its own and races you against your
best time — exactly like a climb. No buttons, no setup.

## Features

- ⏱️ **Live PR racing** on every segment — real-time delta vs. your best time.
- 📍 **Make your own segments (A → B)** — mark start and finish live with the Lap
  button, for any stretch of road, not just climbs.
- 🔁 **Automatic re-detection** — saved segments (and climbs) are timed automatically
  every time you pass through them, in the right direction. Nothing to press.
- 🧗 **Smart climb detection** — climbs are found for you: from the Karoo's native
  climb data when navigating a route, and from a gradient-based detector when riding free.
- 🗺️ **Fully offline** — detection, timing and PRs all run on the device; no account and no data connection needed while riding.
- 💾 **Local history** — your segments, climbs and attempts stay on the device.

## Download & Install

Download the latest **`climber-pr-<version>.apk`** from the
[**Releases page**](../../releases/latest).

**Karoo 3 (no PC needed):**
1. On your phone, long-press the APK download link above.
2. Choose **Share → Hammerhead Companion** app.
3. Confirm the install on the Karoo.

**Karoo 2:**
1. Enable USB debugging on the Karoo and connect it to a PC.
2. Run: `adb install climber-pr-<version>.apk`

After installing, add the **Live Segment** field to a screen profile. Detection runs
only while a ride is recording.

## Updating

When a new version is released, long-tap the app icon on the Karoo's main menu and
select **Update**, or download the new APK and install it over the top — your saved
climbs and your Premium activation are kept.
